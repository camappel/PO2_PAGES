Observed measurements

Dots are values recorded in the workbooks. No values are interpolated between samples. Hover over or select a dot for its source and sample details.

Both plots use the ages supplied by each study, shown here in ka BP. Age models have not yet been reviewed or aligned. Sample depth is the distance into the core, not the water depth at the seafloor.

Different fields have different units and meanings. Oxygen-labelled workbook fields are shown as the source reports them; metals, isotopes, assemblages and other proxies are not automatically converted to dissolved oxygen.

DATA & METHODS

How to read this prototype

What is included

Source records come from the workbooks in the project’s Proxy files folder. The dashboard reads the Data Collection sheet, its header row, unit row, core ID, first reference, age and numeric measurement columns.

Each workbook remains a separate source record, even when several files describe the same core. The name of the original workbook is shown throughout.

Interpretation rules

Age values labelled years before present are displayed as thousands of years before present (ka BP). Blank values and text “NaN” are excluded from charts. Each measurement retains the unit written in its workbook.

Coverage counts describe compiled files and measurements, not independent oxygen reconstructions or complete global sampling.

Profiles, registry and next review

Core profiles explain each registry entry using its identity, cited location evidence and linked workbooks. This public preview includes only 64PE406-E1 and its three linked studies. Missing information and curator review remain explicit.

Workbooks containing several cores have their scope flagged; their combined charts are not individual-core series. Missing locations, ambiguous identities, a reviewed proxy dictionary and age-model status remain the next review work.
